# Marketing and copy

The owner's own voice, channels and promises live in a private overlay, not here.

## 1. Editorial voice
* Write in a clear, authoritative, engaging, conversational human voice.
* Avoid generic AI fluff, passive overuse, jargon overload, and repetitive transitional phrases (for example "In today's digital landscape," "delve," "tapestry").
* Use punchy subheadings, bullet points, concise paragraphs, bold key terms, and active verbs.

## 2. Subject focus
* Content covers business strategy, web design, custom development, UX, SEO, and marketing tips with actionable value.
* Journal posts include code a reader can adapt or use.
* Code in posts is tested before publishing and states the WordPress and PHP version it needs.

## 3. The Rule of One
* **One primary reader:** write to a single persona with explicit pain points.
* **One main offer:** one clear call to action per landing page. Use the same CTA wording for the same action everywhere on the page.
* **One core idea:** a single value proposition per asset.

## 4. Growth marketing
* Build end-to-end funnels, landing page conversion strategies, and lead-generation frameworks sized to the business.
* Align landing pages with the ads, social posts, and emails that send traffic to them.
* **Message match:** the language on the traffic source matches the destination page's headline.

## 5. Trust and proof
* Place trust signals (client logos, reviews, metric callouts, star ratings) above the fold and next to conversion points.
* **Only real proof.** Never write placeholder testimonials, invented metrics, or implied client relationships. If proof is missing, flag the gap and suggest how to get it.
* Label concept and demo projects as demos.

## 6. Content repurposing
* **Create once, distribute everywhere:** turn a long-form article into a short video script, a social carousel, a newsletter feature, and an inline FAQ.
* When cross-posting (DEV.to and similar), set the canonical URL to the original on the owner's own site.


## 7. Social content
* For client work, draft platform-tailored copy for LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Instagram, and YouTube that builds authority and drives organic clicks.
* **Platform-native:** punchy, hook-driven text for X; structured professional insight for LinkedIn; visual formats for Instagram.
* On Reddit and developer communities, lead with something useful. No drive-by link drops.

## 8. Email
* The newsletter is occasional. Send only when there's a new post or release worth reading.
* Subject lines say what's inside. No bait.
* Every send has one link that matters.
