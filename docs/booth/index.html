While you record

  • One listener. Talk to one person, not a microphone.
  • Short lines slower than long ones. Leave a beat after.
  • Lift one underlined word per sentence. Let the ending settle.
  • Breathe in, let a little out, then speak. No gasp.
  • Stumble? Stop, breathe, R, go again from the top.
  • Same distance every take: one side-turned fist from the mic.

Warm-up and mic check

Room tone: records 3 seconds of silence and reports the noise floor. Under −55 dB is good; over −45 dB means find a quieter spot or move closer.
